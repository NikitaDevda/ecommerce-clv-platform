# Power BI Dashboard Design Specifications

## Page Layouts & Navigation
- **Page 1: Executive Summary** - High-level KPIs (Total CLV, Active Customers, AOV, Predicted 6M Revenue).
- **Page 2: Customer Segmentation** - Treemap & Scatter plot breaking down revenue by RFM segments.
- **Page 3: CLV Analytics** - Acquisition channel ROI and cohort retention curves.
- **Page 4: Customer Health Monitoring** - Risk gauges and filterable tables for High-Risk High-Value accounts.
- **Page 5: Retention Strategy Dashboard** - Operational CRM export views for marketing automation.
